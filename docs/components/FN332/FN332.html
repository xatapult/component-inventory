 Schaffner FN332-1/05 - Performance EMC/RFI Filter 

A Schaffner FN332-1/05 is a compact single-phase EMC/EMI line filter designed for general-purpose mains interference suppression, providing 1 A current handling at 250 VAC with a single-stage filtering network and FASTON quick-connect terminals for easy chassis mounting. It reduces conducted noise from DC up to 400 Hz, making it suitable for a wide range of electronic, industrial, and appliance applications.
